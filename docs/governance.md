# Governance — intended direction

VECTRA is currently an independent early-stage project maintained by its initiator. No foundation, board, or formal institutional governance structure currently exists.

## Long-term intention

As the project and contributor community mature, the intention is to explore an independent international foundation.

Potential responsibilities could include:
- Stewardship of project trademarks and assets
- Transparent project governance
- Community representation
- Financial and operational transparency
- Security and release oversight
- Long-term infrastructure sustainability

## Near-term practice

Until a foundation exists:
- Publish technical decisions and roadmap changes
- Explain how maintainership decisions are made
- Document contribution requirements
- Disclose conflicts of interest where relevant
- Avoid implying institutional endorsement or adoption

The future legal structure and jurisdiction require separate research and professional advice.
